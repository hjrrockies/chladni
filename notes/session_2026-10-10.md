1. **Margin rule, measured before designing it** (`notes/margin_rule_offline.md`).
   - `met` holds exactly when R >= 2.
   - On the references' basis (A), all 9 count defects at 1e-10 and 1e-8 sit on levels that miss
     `prec`. A T = 2 rule ("an unmet level's count is flagged too") catches them all, and its
     extra flags land only on levels that already report a shortfall.
   - The HEAD re-run of `count_ratio` qualifies this: on deliberately STARVED bases a double can be
     read as simple at a met level (R up to 18). T = 2 does not see that; basis sufficiency or the
     planner has to.
   - Basis adequacy is the dominant limit: basis A misses 1e-10 on 40% of levels.
   - **Suggestion:** Phase 3 shrinks to "unmet ⇒ count flagged", and the planner's enrichment
     moves up.

     `SUITE_RTOL`, and **0** at a 1e-10-sized one.

   - **Suggestion, for review:** `FINEST_PREC = 1e-10` for the suite, with the evidence modules
