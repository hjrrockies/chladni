## The overnight crash (2026-10-13, 00:47)

**Cause:** memory. Two concurrent 8-worker runs of the comparison benchmark held 39.6 GB on a
16 GB Mac, and the kernel's watchdog panicked; the Mac rebooted at 01:02. Found in
`/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`: the JetsamEvent at 00:47, and the panic report written at
08:14.

**Effects:** no results were lost. Since then the benchmark runs one job per process, logs peak
memory, and caps each job at 4 GB.

## The douse comparison (`benchmarks/chladni_vs_lappy.py` in douse)

The first runs were **unconstrained, by mistake**: `maximize_ratio` got no `ConstraintSet`, so an
optimizer could drive an edge to zero, a shape douse is set up never to visit. N = 6, k = 2,
seed 1 stalled there three times. Rerun with douse's own constraints
(`MinEdgeLength` + `ClosingBudget`, as in `ratio_multistart.py`), all 36 runs completed. There
were no errors, no unresolved spectra and no refusal stalls
(`benchmarks/run/chladni_vs_lappy_constrained.jsonl`):

| N, k | chladni | lappy | per-run wall, chladni / lappy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4, 1 | 2.5 | 2.5 (to ~1e-10) | 111-203 / 18-27 s |
| 4, 2 | 35/11 | 35/11 (to ~2e-10) | 448-477 / 89-109 s |
| 5, 1 | 2.52683873 | 2.52683873 (to ~1e-9) | 190-547 / 184-204 s |
| 5, 2 | 3.19087, 3.19074, 3.19095 | 3.19087, 3.18489, 3.19120 | 1102-1267 / 135-246 s |
| 6, 1 | 2.534006, 2.533993, 2.533922 | 2.533996, 2.533904, 2.533933 | 262-273 / 246-263 s |
| 6, 2 | 3.19755, 3.18235, 3.19175 | 3.19514, 3.18016, 3.19632 | 800-1944 / 180-325 s |

**How to read the differences.** Where the N = 5-6 results differ, both runs hit the 80-iteration
cap, so the end points depend on the path taken. Earlier same-shape cross-checks put
solver-to-solver disagreement at 1e-11 or better. The one exception was lappy, about 1e-9 off on
a short-edge shape, where four independent chladni bases agree to about 1e-11. The constraints
also fixed lappy's two short N = 5, k = 2 runs (from 3.06-3.09 to 3.185-3.191).

**Speed.** chladni matches lappy at N = 6, k = 1 and is 4-9x slower on k = 2, near the cluster. A
cold N = 6 re-solve is 86% search, run as 186 serial tension evaluations with 10 cores idle;
certification is 13%.
