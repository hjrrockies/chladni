and are not repeated here; this records what the session was FOR, the pattern across it, and the

## Where the pipeline stands

**No known silent wrong totals and no known lost eigenvalues on any measured grid -- ON
macOS/ACCELERATE.** 216 right, 0 wrong, 35 declined over five split-degeneracy grids (`50`, `65`,
`125`, `145`, `170` x `pi^2`) at five precisions and eleven bands, unexercisable cells excluded;
the roster certified at every precision on the ladder. The contract, and what it does NOT
guarantee, is in `docs/design/search.md`'s As-built section.

**The platform qualifier was added after the fact and is the session's loose end.** CI on
`ubuntu-latest` with `scipy-openblas32` reads `N65-1e-06-b2.6` as 3 of 4, silently; every other
cell agrees. The claim was written and merged without it, because every measurement in this
session was taken on one machine and nothing prompted the question until CI ran the same grids
on another. A result measured on one platform is a claim about that platform -- section 7's
lesson in a coordinate nobody was watching.

Built: attribution (a branch the read counts inside is owned once), `modes(x)` on the `Objective`
with `MPSPencil.interior_image`, Phase D's interior Gram angle behind `phase_d=` and off,

## The pattern, and it is the session's main result

**Five plausible remedies were proposed by `TODO.md` or by me, and measurement said four of them
were aimed at the wrong thing.** Not "needed tuning" -- aimed at the wrong thing.

| proposed | measured | section |
|---|---|---|
| hand a non-settled seed to the cover phase | 9 wrong answers, incl. 176 of 6, 3.4x cost | 34 |
| freeze the rank to remove the discontinuity | 2.0e11x floor inflation on a wide window | 35 |
| flag refiner rejections that are not `not_a_minimum`/`endpoint` | 24 false alarms, 0 true positives | 37 |
| the Weyl deficit as the starved-basis alarm | insensitive: 0.91 missing none, 0.87 missing six | 38 |
| `findability` as the starved-basis alarm | flat across a 4.7x change in basis size | 38 |

Each was reasonable on its face, and each had a story. What killed them was measuring the whole
population rather than the cases that prompted the proposal -- section 7's lesson, arriving four
more times. The refiner-rejection alarm is the cleanest example: it was derived from a rescan of
cells that were ALREADY suspicious, and the reason distribution over all 179 rejections inverts
it.

The corollary is worth stating because it cuts the other way too: **the three that survived
survived on the same instrument.** The standoff went to 0.3 because a five-grid sweep showed 216
right / 0 wrong and a plateau from 0.3 to 0.5; attribution shipped because the grids moved the
right way; Phase D's angle did NOT ship because the grids did not move at all.

## Two things that were passing for the wrong reason

Both were instrument defects rather than solver defects, and both were found by fixing something
else.

* **A `3 of 3` that was two errors cancelling.** At `50*pi^2`, band 0.3, prec 1e-12 the read at
  member 1 counted member 2 inside AND member 2 was reported from its own bracket -- a double
  count -- while member 3 was never located. Attribution removed the double count and made the
  miss visible, which briefly looked like a regression.
* **The cluster grid was scoring twelve cells its basis cannot resolve.** At `prec = 1e-14`,
  bands 0.3 and 0.5 dial the members closer than the multiplicity resolution (2.99e-15 against
  7.11e-15 at `50*pi^2`), so no counting rule can be right there and whatever the cell reported
  was luck. `TODO.md` section 8's precondition rule, learned by a fourth tier.

## Errors made in this session, and how each was caught

Recorded because the session's own conclusions depend on the corrections holding.

* **The Weyl measurement was wrong the first time.** `weyl_count_poly` counts WITH multiplicity
  and I audited one entry per distinct LEVEL, on a domain whose spectrum is mostly double. That
  alone reports +4.70 on the true spectrum, and I had written a confident story around it --
  "anti-correlated, reads highest on the correct answer". Caught only because the user asked me
  to explain the construction carefully. The conclusion survived; the mechanism stated for it did
  not, and the corrected reason (structural insensitivity) is the better one.
* **The first Phase D synthetic was malformed** -- all four mode directions identical, so of
  course it collapsed. Fixing it exposed the real case: the ambiguous eigenspace is NOT benign
  under the reach rule, which is now section 36.2 and a correction to `search.md`.
* **`_SIDE_STANDOFF` was first set from a sweep on ONE cell.** It shipped at 0.1 with a note
  saying it wanted the five grids; the five grids then said 0.3. The note is why it got fixed.
* **`findability` was dismissed on a bad inference.** I read its flat readings under basis
  starvation as evidence it detects nothing useful. It keys on the PLATEAU and catchment width;
  starvation raises the FLOOR. Different mechanism, correct reading, wrong conclusion -- withdrawn
  in section 38.
* **A merge run as `git merge -F -`**, which does not read stdin the way `git commit` does. The
  `checkout main` in the same command had already succeeded, leaving the tree on `main` without
  the work for one confusing minute. Nothing lost; chaining a checkout with a merge is what made
  a partial failure land somewhere surprising.

## Three corrections to `search.md`, all found by measuring what it asserted

The distinct-pair bound is a FLOOR THAT MOVES with `1/sqrt(n_int)`, not the constant 0.10 (the
rectangle's own 0.1402 already exceeded it). "Even at two-thirds of a gap" probes PAST THE
MIDPOINT and measures the neighbour. The ambiguous eigenspace is not benign under the reach rule.
Plus the ATTIC the spec specifies and the implementation reaches by recomputation instead.

## What is open, and why it was left

* **The rank policy.** Deferred to basis planning by decision, with both horns measured
  (sections 34, 35). It is one decision the code lets three places make separately -- the window,
  the rank and the floor -- and a planner chooses the triple.
* **`MARGIN = 1.5`.** The only one of the three inherited constants with correctness at stake,
  and the real question is whether stopping at isolation is sound off the rectangle rather than
  what the number should be.
* **`findability`.** Costs +16% to +67% of a solve. Guards a real, observed production failure
  (the 21.9/25.0/133.1 triangle returning a non-consecutive spectrum) that no floor check or
  counting test catches. Open question before wiring: its criterion assumes a UNIFORM scan of
  step `dk`, and this bracketer is branch-and-bound with exclusion.
* **Phase D's angle.** Built, correct, consulted 115 times, changes nothing. Either construct a
  case where the two rules differ, or accept it as insurance against a case this roster does not
  contain -- and say which.
