## What shipped in `src/`

* **`reg="tikhonov_qr"`, and the literal `"tikhonov"`** -- a penalty in place of the rank
  truncation, so no rank is chosen and none can flip. An OPTION, not the default.
  `_regularize.py` carries the derivation; `notes/tikhonov_derivations.pdf` carries the maths.
  The literal form is an oracle only: `tau*I` makes the stack full rank but leaves the interior
  block's rank to LAPACK, which decides it per `lam`.
* **A floor-aware guard on the cache wall slope** (`search._slope_from_cache`). The cone identity
  assumed `sigma` reaches zero; on a well with a floor it read `2*floor/(hi-lo)` -- 8.45e6 against
  a true 0.02-0.08 -- which collapsed the side search's exclusion radius and made one eigenvalue
  come back as two levels. The guard fires only where the blind estimate exceeds the floor-aware
  one by 100x, so the calibrated slope is unchanged bit for bit everywhere else. An unconditional
  version flipped four calibrated tests; that is why it is a guard.

## What was DECIDED (2026-09-23)

**The penalty regularization is the intended default**, replacing the threshold rank, work
remaining and all. `TODO.md` section 4 holds the decision and the gates; the reason is that a
threshold rank leaves a decision to rounding, that has already cost a real answer on Linux
(`notes/session_2026-09-19.md`), and no tuning removes it.

The evidence AGAINST is deliberately left standing in `notes/smooth_filter.md` section 8, because
the decision has to live with it.

## What was measured, and where it lives

`notes/smooth_filter.md` is the record; sections 9 and 10 are this session's.

* **9** -- what landed, and why the QR form rather than the literal one.
* **10, 10.4** -- the shipped `rtol = sqrt(prec*eps)` rule is mismatched to a penalty: a
  truncation's floor is set by the basis, a penalty's floor IS `tau ~ sqrt(prec)`, so `floor/prec`
  grows like `1/sqrt(prec)` and crosses 1 between 1e-12 and 1e-14.
  Figure: `notes/fig_penalty_prec_limit.png`.
* **10.5** -- gate 0, partially run. At `prec = 1e-14` the shipped rule is **silently wrong on all
  18 cells**; `tau = C*prec` turns that into declines. At 1e-10 the penalty is clean.
* **10.6** -- rule-free `tau` sweep. **A feasible `tau` EXISTS at 1e-12** -- the clean band is
  `tau` in [0.007, 0.05] `prec`, 12 of 12, matching the threshold -- and **none exists at 1e-14**,
  where declines and wrong totals meet with no gap. At 1e-6 and 1e-8 the penalty BEATS the
  threshold (12 of 12 against 11 and a decline).

Reproduce with `notes/smooth_filter_tau.py` (`noise`, `rules`, `sweep`, `band`),
`notes/fig_penalty_prec_limit.py`, and the section-7 runners `notes/smooth_filter_{wide,lshape,
declines}.py`. The order-2 runners behind 10.1-10.3 were scratch and are not promoted.

## The lesson worth carrying

**A bound derived from one configuration did not transfer, twice, and a cheap direct sweep beat
it both times.** The order-2 sweep's "floor <= 0.2 prec is safe" became "C <= 0.14" for order 1;
the real band is [0.007, 0.05], 5-10x lower, and section 10.5 concluded "not ready at 1e-12" from
sampling only the derived window. Sweep first in this area.
